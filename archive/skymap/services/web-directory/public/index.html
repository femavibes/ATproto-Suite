About

ATlas is a community-building tool for the ATmosphere designed to refine content feeds by integrating voluntary location data. By opting in, people allow their accounts to be labeled with geographic metadata, enabling developers to build region-specific feeds that more accurately highlight the needs and interests of local communities.

Improved user experience on the ATmosphere does not need to come at the expense of user safety. To ensure privacy and safety, ATlas adheres to these core principles:

Voluntary Participation:

Location labeling is strictly "opt-in"; no user data is processed without explicit consent.

Privacy Protections:

We do not apply location labels to private citizens without their consent.

Public and Private Infrastructure:

While private individuals remain unlabeled, ATlas provides labels accounts of government officials, public institutions, and private-sector entities.

City-Level Focus:

To protect user security, labels are restricted to the city level. ATlas will never share granular street-level data or real-time tracking information.

ATlas lets you add hidden location labels (3) to your Bluesky account for custom feeds and community discovery.

Your account will also be placed into a corresponding ATProto list. These lists can be used by custom feeds to include your posts in your local area.

This repository enables feedmakers to easily create local feeds for any topic.

ATlas also lets you see your mutuals on the map! And more to come...
