What does adding a location label do?

When you add a location label (one primary and up to two secondary locations), several things happen:

1. ATProto List Membership

Your account is added to our database and included in an ATProto list for that location. These lists can be used by feed developers to create custom local feeds, helping you discover and connect with people in your area.

2. Custom Location Label

Your account receives a shadow label with custom location metadata, adding persistence to your location information across the ATmosphere.
