## Key Statistics from 1000-post sample:

**Note:** These statistics are based on a 1,000-post sample from Bluesky's firehose and may not represent the entire platform.

- 47.8% are replies
- Embeds: 16.4% external links, 12.1% images, 7% quotes, 0.7% videos
- 29% of accounts have labels
- Most common labels: !no-unauthenticated (privacy), bridged accounts (federation)
- Average post length: ~99 characters
- Follower distribution: median 423, mean 2,376, max 165,876
- Average 2.5 facets (links/mentions/tags) per post with facets
- 3.6% use custom domain handles
- Image alt text: Only ~16% of images have non-empty alt text
